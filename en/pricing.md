## Choosing a Plan

- **Free** — Quick evaluation, no credit card required
- **Starter** — Individual developers and small projects
- **Professional** — Quant teams that need professional financial data, news, and higher request limits
- **All HK-Shares / All A-Shares / All US-Shares** — For users who need Professional-level capabilities with a stronger focus on one stock market; alongside professional financial and news data, one request retrieves the complete market snapshot and one subscription continuously delivers real-time updates for every symbol
- **Enterprise** — For users who need complete symbol coverage across multiple stock markets, with full-market queries and subscriptions for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and US stocks, plus higher connection and request limits
